## 1. Gap Statement & Context  

Design and evaluate an integrated Augmented Reality–Artificial Intelligence (AR‑AI) digital learning environment for construction technology students that simultaneously targets **cognitive understanding** (through immersive, visualised content) and **psychomotor skill acquisition** (through AI‑driven practice and feedback).  

- An AR‑based interactive module for a Building Construction II course has been shown to be valid and reliable for vocational pedagogy, confirming its capacity to support **cognitive learning** in construction technology [@KESAHAN DAN KEBOLEHPERCAYAAN PEMBANGUNAN MODUL INTERAKTIF BERASASKAN TEKNOLOGI AUGMENTED REALITY BAGI KURSUS PEMBINAAN BANGUNAN II].  
- AI instructional tools have been demonstrated to **enhance psychomotor performance** of construction‑technology students in a Malaysian vocational college [@828777f8-da7c-4b96-b650-38a74cac8106].  

Although each technology independently improves a distinct domain of learning, **no existing study** has combined AR (cognitive scaffold) with AI (psychomotor support) into a single multimodal environment for construction technology education. This represents a clear research gap that warrants systematic investigation.

## 3. Methodological Recommendations  

| Recommendation | Rationale (linked to evidence) | Suggested Design Elements |
|----------------|--------------------------------|---------------------------|
| **a. Mixed‑methods quasi‑experimental design** that measures both cognitive gains (e.g., concept‑mapping, MCQ scores) and psychomotor performance (e.g., task‑time, accuracy on simulated construction activities). | Extends the assessment approaches used separately in the AR validation study [@KESAHAN DAN KEBOLEHPERCAYAAN PEMBANGUNAN MODUL INTERAKTIF BERASASKAN TEKNOLOGI AUGMENTED REALITY BAGI KURSUS PEMBINAAN BANGUNAN II] and the AI psychomotor study [@828777f8-da7c-4b96-b650-38a74cac8106]. | • Pre‑post cognitive test battery.<br>• Objective psychomotor metrics captured via motion sensors or instrumented workstations.<br>• Triangulation with student reflections and focus groups. |
| **b. Integrated AR‑AI platform architecture** where AR visualisations are synchronized with AI‑generated adaptive feedback. | The AR module proved reliable for presenting construction concepts [@KESAHAN DAN KEBOLEHPERCAYAAN PEMBANGUNAN MODUL INTERAKTIF BERASASKAN TEKNOLOGI AUGMENTED REALITY BAGI KURSUS PEMBINAAN BANGUNAN II]; AI was effective for skill feedback [@828777f8-da7c-4b96-b650-38a74cac8106]. | • AR overlays of structural components during hands‑on tasks.<br>• AI algorithms that analyse sensor data (e.g., tool trajectory) and deliver real‑time hints or corrective cues within the AR view. |
| **c. Sequential learning scaffold**: begin with AR‑mediated concept exploration, followed by AI‑guided practice sessions, and culminate in a blended AR‑AI performance task. | Aligns with the separate success of AR for cognition and AI for psychomotor skill, proposing a **sequenced integration** to bridge the identified gap. | • Phase 1: AR‑only tutorials.<br>• Phase 2: AI‑driven drills without AR.<br>• Phase 3: Integrated AR‑AI task where students apply concepts while receiving AI feedback. |
| **d. Reliability and validity checks for the combined system** using Cronbach’s α for cognitive items and inter‑rater reliability for psychomotor scoring, echoing validation approaches of the original AR study [@KESAHAN DAN KEBOLEHPERCAYAAN PEMBANGUNAN MODUL INTERAKTIF BERASASKAN TEKNOLOGI AUGMENTED REALITY BAGI KURSUS PEMBINAAN BANGUNAN II] and the AI study [@828777f8-da7c-4b96-b650-38a74cac8106]. | Ensures the integrated environment meets the same rigorous standards demonstrated separately for each technology. | • Pilot testing with a small cohort.<br>• Statistical analysis of measurement consistency. |
| **e. Qualitative inquiry** (e.g., semi‑structured interviews) to capture student perceptions of **cognitive‑psychomotor synergy** within the AR‑AI environment. | Qualitative inquiry into user experience within the integrated setting will reveal emergent affordances or challenges not captured by quantitative metrics alone [@828777f8-da7c-4b96-b650-38a74cac8106]. | • Interview protocol focusing on perceived integration, confidence, and transferability of skills. |
