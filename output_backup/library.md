2. **Infrastructure & Policy Gaps:** The absence of institutional AI governance policies and limited access to high-performance computational hardware in vocational workshops create significant friction, dampening the transition from positive student attitudes toward actual usage in technical problem-solving workflows.

### Synthesis Notes: Connection to Kolej Vokasional (KV) Studies

#### 1. Bridge to Practical Construction Modules (*Teknologi Pembinaan*)
* **Contextual Alignment:** In Kolej Vokasional (KV) environments—specifically within the *Teknologi Pembinaan* (Construction Technology) program—learning outcomes require a balance of conceptual understanding (e.g., structural calculations, building code interpretations) and physical execution (e.g., masonry, carpentry, site surveying).
* **Synthesis:** Baharin’s (2025) findings on Performance Expectancy directly mirror the operational needs of KV Construction Technology students. Generative AI tools (e.g., custom prompts for structural material estimations or building safety code analysis) can lower cognitive load during design and pre-construction planning, directly reinforcing hands-on workshop application.

#### 2. Mitigating the Risk of Practical Skill Atrophy
* **Critical Distinction:** KV curricula are structured around 70% practical workshop training and 30% academic theory. Baharin highlights the risk of procedural dependency on AI.
* **Pedagogical Solution for KV:** To prevent passive reliance during practical modules, AI tools in KV programs must be structured as *co-pilots for diagnostic troubleshooting* (e.g., generating fault-tree analyses for concrete mixing errors or structural defects) rather than replacement engines for manual technical execution.

#### 3. Institutional Facilitating Conditions in Malaysian KV System
* **Infrastructure & Policy Alignment:** The paper emphasizes that *Facilitating Conditions* dictate success. In the context of the Malaysian Ministry of Education (MOE) TVET division (BPLTV), implementing Generative AI within KV institutions requires formalizing institutional prompt literacy frameworks, enhancing campus Wi-Fi/hardware capabilities, and upskilling KV lecturers in AI-assisted formative assessment.
